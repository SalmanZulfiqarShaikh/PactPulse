The Disclosing Party and the Receiving Party are collectively referred to as the "Parties" and individually as a "Party".
WHEREAS:
A. The Disclosing Party is developing "Pact Pulse", a proprietary artificial intelligence-driven legaltech platform designed for automated contract analysis, risk evaluation, and clause review tailored for legal professionals.
B. The Parties wish to engage in business and technical discussions regarding early-stage testing, pilot demonstration, evaluation, or integration of Pact Pulse (the "Permitted Purpose").
C. In connection with the Permitted Purpose, the Disclosing Party may disclose proprietary, confidential, and technical information to the Receiving Party under the terms set forth herein.
NOW, THEREFORE, the Parties agree as follows:
1. Confidential Information
"Confidential Information" refers to any proprietary information disclosed by the Disclosing Party to the Receiving Party, whether orally, in writing, electronically, or through platform demonstrations, including but not limited to:

* The architecture, machine learning models, algorithms, workflows, prompt engineering frameworks, user interfaces, feature sets, and source/object code of Pact Pulse.
* Product roadmaps, unreleased features, benchmarking metrics, technical specifications, and system integration designs.
* Business strategies, marketing plans, pricing structures, and prospective commercial arrangements.

2. Exclusions from Confidential Information
Confidential Information does not include information that:

* Is or becomes publicly known through no breach of this Agreement by the Receiving Party.
* Was already known to the Receiving Party prior to disclosure, without confidentiality restrictions, as evidenced by written records.
* Is lawfully received from an independent third party without breach of any non-disclosure obligation.
* Is independently developed by the Receiving Party without reference to or reliance upon Pact Pulse's proprietary materials.

3. Obligations of the Receiving Party

* Duty of Confidentiality: The Receiving Party shall hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence and exercise at least the same degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential materials of like nature, but not less than a reasonable degree of care.
* Limited Use: The Receiving Party shall use the Confidential Information solely for evaluating or piloting Pact Pulse for the Permitted Purpose and shall not use it for any commercial exploitation outside of this collaboration.
* Non-Reverse Engineering: The Receiving Party shall not reverse-engineer, decompile, disassemble, extract source code, copy workflows, or replicate the proprietary logic of Pact Pulse.
* Need-to-Know Restriction: Access shall be restricted strictly to partners, associates, or employees who have a legitimate need to know for the Permitted Purpose and who are bound by confidentiality obligations no less restrictive than those contained herein.

4. Professional Privilege and Client Data
The Parties recognize that the Receiving Party is an advocate/law firm subject to the legal professional privilege under Article 9 of the Qanun-e-Shahadat Order, 1984. The Disclosing Party does not require, solicit, or intend to access any privileged client records or confidential case files of the Receiving Party during platform demonstrations, unless anonymized or covered by separate data processing protocols.
5. Proprietary Rights
All rights, title, and interest in and to Pact Pulse, including patents, copyrights, trade secrets, trademarks, and associated intellectual property rights, remain the exclusive property of the Disclosing Party. Nothing in this Agreement grants any express or implied license or transfer of ownership.
6. Term and Termination

* This Agreement governs disclosures made within two (2) years from the Effective Date.
* The confidentiality obligations under this Agreement shall survive the termination or expiration of discussions for a period of three (3) years from the date of disclosure; provided that any trade secrets or proprietary source/model code shall remain confidential indefinitely or for the maximum duration permitted under the laws of Pakistan.

7. Return or Destruction of Materials
Upon written request by the Disclosing Party, the Receiving Party shall promptly return or certify the secure destruction of all tangible and electronic embodiments of Confidential Information, retaining only such copies as are strictly required by statutory record-keeping obligations or standard non-accessible automated IT backups.
8. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

* This Agreement is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Islamic Republic of Pakistan.
* In the event of any dispute arising out of or relating to this Agreement, the Parties shall first attempt amicable settlement. Failing settlement within thirty (30) days, the dispute shall be referred to arbitration in Karachi under the provisions of the Arbitration Act, 1940.
* Subject to arbitration, the competent courts at Karachi shall have exclusive jurisdiction.

9. Injunctive Relief
The Receiving Party acknowledges that monetary damages may be inadequate to remedy a breach of this Agreement, and the Disclosing Party shall be entitled to seek temporary or permanent injunctive relief in the courts of Karachi to prevent unauthorized disclosure or misuse.
